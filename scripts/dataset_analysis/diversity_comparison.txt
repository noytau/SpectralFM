==========================================================================================
METRIC EXPLANATIONS
==========================================================================================

Cosine Similarity: Measures direction alignment between samples.
  PROBLEM: All non-negative vectors have high cosine sim (>0.3).
  High mean = samples point in similar directions.

Angular Distance: arccos(cosine_sim)/π. Linearizes cosine sim.
  Spreads the values more evenly across [0, 1].
  Higher = more diverse directions.

Euclidean Distance: Captures BOTH direction AND magnitude.
  Two signals with same shape but different amplitude → different.
  Higher mean = more diverse dataset. More informative than cosine.

PCA Effective Rank: Information-theoretic dimensionality.
  exp(entropy(eigenvalues)). Higher = data spans more dimensions.
  Low effective rank = data is mostly along a few directions.

PCA Components for 90%: How many PCs to explain 90% of variance.
  More components needed = data is more spread in feature space.

Vendi Score: Information-theoretic diversity from similarity kernel.
  Range: 1 (all identical) to N (perfectly diverse).
  Based on eigenvalue entropy of the cosine similarity matrix.

1-NN Distance: How far each sample is from its nearest neighbor.
  Higher = samples are more spread out (no tight clusters).
  Low = many near-duplicate or very similar samples.

Std of Sample Means: How much individual sample means vary.
  Higher = more variety in signal energy levels across samples.

Range of Sample Means: Max - Min of per-sample means.
  Higher = dataset covers a wider range of signal types.

Unique Batches: How many source batch files are represented.
  9 batches total (batch0-batch8). All 9 = good coverage.

Batch Count Std: How evenly samples are distributed across batches.
  Lower = more uniform sampling from each batch.
  Higher = some batches over/under-represented.

Unique Stacks: Number of distinct stacks (index // 10).
  Each stack groups ~10 consecutive spectrograms.
  More unique stacks = samples from more diverse positions.

Stack/Index Range: Span of stack/spectrogram indices covered.
  Wider = samples drawn from across the full measurement range.
  Narrow = samples clustered in one region.
