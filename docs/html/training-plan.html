SpectralFM — data2vec-audio on NOVA spectra

3AE + d2v Joint Training Plan

Built bottom-up: a pure self-distillation FE pretrain (no decoder), then FE and projection trained together with a reconstruction decoder attached, then projection fine-tuned further across three capacities, then the transformer — each step freezing and building on the previous one's checkpoint — joined in a final full fine-tune.

Step 0 option: train FE + projection together from random init (transformer still frozen from base_libri), rather than freezing projection at base_libri from the start. Same d2v-only loss, no decoder either way — just widens which components receive gradients in Step 0 itself.

λ_d2v is new. Every step's d2v term now carries an explicit weight, same pattern as the recon λs — it doesn't exist in the model yet (today's forward() writes the regression loss with no lambda multiplier). Small addition, same shape of change as the existing recon lambdas.
